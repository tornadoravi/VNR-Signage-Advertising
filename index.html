    A LITTLE CLARITY

    Before we
    begin.

    A few useful answers before your signage project gets started.

    Have a different question? Call VNR
    What kinds of signage can I discuss with VNR?

    Our documented work includes 3D LED letters, UV backlit and glow boards, reception and backdrop logos, printed flex, embossed house names, P10 LED displays, LED welcome arches, LED menu boards, ACP elevation and embedded signs, reflective signage, industrial identification and wayfinding. The Solutions section also lists other services from our Google business description.

    Is a flex board the same as an LED letter board?

    No. Printed flex signage carries printed graphics, while LED letter boards use illuminated lettering. The Phonic Elite project shown here is a printed flex board; it is not an LED letter or ACP board.

    What should I have ready before calling?

    Your business name or logo, the approximate sign dimensions, the installation location and any preferred finish or reference. These are useful starting points for discussing the design and requirements.

    Are the project photos and reviews genuine?

    OUR Projects contains 119 real photographs and owner-made photo montages from 35 Google business updates, organised into 35 company and project galleries. Fabrication and delivery views are distinguished from completed installations. The Clients section links named businesses, brands and institutions to their corresponding work; unnamed and residential projects remain in the full showcase. The hero and service graphics are original illustrations, not installation photographs. Reviews are excerpts linked to their original Google reviews, and the rating is a dated snapshot rather than a live feed.

    How do I get a price or a completion date?

    Call +91 90805 07526 to discuss the sign type, dimensions, materials and installation requirements. Pricing and timing need to be confirmed directly for your particular project.
